# 🍽️ Swiggy Restaurant Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This project presents a comprehensive Power BI dashboard analyzing Swiggy restaurant data across multiple Indian cities. It uncovers operational trends, customer preferences, and performance metrics to support strategic decision-making for food delivery platforms.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Visualize restaurant distribution, ratings, and cuisine diversity
- Explore correlations between price, delivery time, and customer satisfaction
- Provide actionable business recommendations for growth and optimization

## 📂 Dataset
The dataset includes:
- 📍 City and Area
- 🍱 Food Type and Cuisine Variety
- ⏱️ Delivery Time
- 💰 Restaurant Price
- ⭐ Ratings and Total Reviews
- 📊 Restaurant Count and Location

## 📊 Dashboard Features
Each task was translated into a visual insight using Power BI:

| Task | Description | Visualization |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| 1 | Top 10 areas with most restaurants | Stacked Column Chart |
| 2 | Most food types served per city | Stacked Column Chart |
| 3 | Restaurants with avg rating > 4.5 | Pie Chart |
| 4 | Correlation: Avg Rating vs Price, Total Ratings, Delivery Time | Gauge Meter |
| 5 | Correlation: Price vs Avg Rating | Gauge Meter |
| 6 | Total restaurants per city | Pie Chart |
| 7 | Price distribution by city | Pie Chart |
| 8 | Delivery time by city | Stacked Column Chart |
| 9 | Cuisine variety by city | Pie Chart |
| 10 | Area-wise restaurant count | Line + Stacked Column Chart |
| 11 | Variable correlation analysis | Line Chart |
| 12 | Customer feedback analysis | Line Chart |
| 13 | Restaurant location mapping | Geographical Map |
| 14 | Business recommendations | Text Box Summary |

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **Kolkata, Mumbai, and Pune** lead in restaurant density.
- **Delivery time** shows strong negative correlation with ratings.
- **Mumbai** generates the highest revenue; **Surat** lags behind.
- **Ahmedabad** has the fastest delivery times; **Chennai** the slowest.
- **Price and total ratings** have weak correlation with average rating.

## 💡 Business Impact
This dashboard enables Swiggy stakeholders to:
- Optimize delivery logistics and pricing strategies
- Enhance customer satisfaction through targeted improvements
- Expand services in high-performing regions
- Refine promotional campaigns based on data-driven insights
